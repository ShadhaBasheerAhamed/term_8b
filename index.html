AI Ethics

Using AI responsibly, fairly and safely.

What is Ethics?

Ethics means principles that help us decide what is right or wrong and fair or unfair.

What is AI Ethics?

AI Ethics means developing and using AI in a responsible, fair and safe way.

⚖️ AI Bias

Unfair AI results can happen when data or human assumptions are biased.

Example: A scholarship AI should not unfairly favour boys or girls.

🔒 Privacy

Personal information should be protected.

Example: A school AI app should keep names, marks and photos safe.

💼 Job Replacement

AI and automation can change the tasks people do. Responsible use considers people and technology together.

📰 Misinformation

AI can produce incorrect information. Check information before believing or sharing it.

Four qualities of ethical AI

QualityMeaningKid example
Human-centricPeople firstAI helps a student understand difficult Maths.
UnbiasedTreat people fairlyScholarship AI judges students fairly.
Data-protectiveKeep personal data safeStudent information is not shared without permission.
SustainableUse resources responsiblyAI uses computing resources carefully.
Memory line: 👥 People first • ⚖️ Fair • 🔒 Protect data • 🌱 Use resources responsibly

Everyday bias examples

Virtual assistants have historically often used female voices, which can be used to discuss assumptions about gender. Search results for “salon” can also reflect patterns or assumptions present in data and society.

Systems Thinking

Look at the whole system, not only one part.

What is a system?

A system is a group of connected parts that work together.

Interconnectedness

Interconnectedness means that parts of a system can affect one another.

💧 Water cycle

Its stages are connected.

🏫 School

Students, teachers, rooms and resources are connected.

🍎 Food chain

A change in one part can affect others.

🚦 Traffic

Vehicles, roads, signals and people affect one another.

Remember: System = connected parts. Systems thinking = understand the connections.

Conditional statements

A condition is a question whose answer can be True or False.

IF = “If this is true, do this.”
age = 15

if age >= 13:
    print("You are a teenager")

Indentation
